    Discovery

    Automatic top listings

    Good matches at or above this score receive ★ Auto top. The full listing must be read and have no review gaps. Applied, archived and closed roles are excluded. Your manually starred roles stay saved when scores change.

    Search results are leads, and may be out of date. Read the full listing before preparing documents. You can paste a role and description using Add a role.

    Scheduled emails & standout alerts

    Checked hourly, including overnight. Only strong matches in your preferred location or Australian remote roles qualify; unresolved requirements stay for review. Quiet when nothing qualifies.

    Daily: up to 10 search queries and 12 listing reads. Extended: up to 24 queries and 32 reads across related titles, Australia-wide employers and job boards. Each run also checks recent Gmail job alerts. Shortlists use confirmed résumé facts and need your review before applying. Previously emailed roles are skipped.

    Optional daytime discovery

    This optional daytime timer only saves leads while the workspace is open. The twice-daily and hourly routines use saved Codex schedules; keep your Mac awake, online and the desktop app running. The first checkbox pauses all delivery; the second pauses standout alerts. Change scheduled times in Scheduled.

    Résumé & cover-letter guardrails

    • Skills, employment facts, dates and qualifiers must match your saved profile.
    • The role and employer must be identified; cover-letter wording must follow the grounded template.
    • PDFs must use black text on A4: up to 3 résumé pages and a 1-page letter, with candidate details intact.
    • Named referees need contact details and your sharing confirmation.
    • Read the employer's application instructions: a brief introduction may be more appropriate than a full cover letter.
    • Keep personal prototypes, proposals, and live workplace achievements separate; never invent savings, adoption or usage numbers.
    • Before sharing, remove confidential employer material, confirm referees' permission, and check every claim and page break yourself.
    • Profile changes, changed PDFs or outdated checks hold delivery until the packet is regenerated.

    Checks run again before Gmail or Drive delivery. Manual sends require the review checkbox. Scheduled delivery is separately opt-in. These rules check against your saved profile; they do not independently verify its truth or guarantee eligibility or a successful application.
